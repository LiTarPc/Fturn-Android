# Fturn 4.3.2 — RAW stability candidate

This candidate restores the full sing-box → authenticated RAW SOCKS bridge used by the verified 4.1.0-raw-debug build. sing-box owns the Android TUN, application/bypass rules, authenticated hotspot and update proxy. Ordinary port-53 DNS is intercepted and sent through RAW over TCP; encrypted external DNS follows its own protocol.

The RAW bridge now admits at most 1024 simultaneous TCP/control handlers, up from its former 256 budget and the 128-handler explicit hotspot introduced in 4.3.1. Go handles the bridge connections; there is no Java worker per established TCP tunnel. Established TCP relays expire after ten minutes without activity or one minute without activity after a half-close; traffic in the remaining direction keeps a half-closed relay alive.

App version: 4.3.2. RAW core: 1.0.1-beta.android.2, Go 1.26.10. TURN core pin unchanged. The user's 4.1.0-raw-debug APK on the second phone has SHA-256 `18da4fc3d770bcdd0043c8edf2c17da05a60da1f3e9f9251ed6e604baa705463`; its bytecode confirms the sing-box hotspot and TCP DNS configuration. The early direct-RAW APK with the same displayed version is a different file.

Local host tests of rawsocks, rawvpn, bypass and turndial pass. The 1024-handler admission/release test and idle/half-close/cancellation regressions pass. Full APK CI, Android bridge checks and installed-candidate/router validation remain required before publication. Passing capacity tests alone does not establish that all reported QUIC or intermittent Internet failures are resolved.

The router independently limits its hev tunnel to 128 sessions and has about 56 MiB RAM available to its OS. Its capacity must be evaluated separately; a phone with 6 GiB RAM does not remove that router limit. No router configuration is changed by this app update.
